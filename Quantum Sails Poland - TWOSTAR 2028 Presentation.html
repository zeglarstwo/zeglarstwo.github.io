Innovation & Performance

Quantum Fusion M Sail Technology

Patented membrane technology providing a competitive edge in all wind conditions.

Membrane Technology

Quantum Fusion M sails are custom designed using 3D modeling. Carbon and aramid fibers are aligned precisely along load paths, delivering unmatched shape retention and stretch resistance.

Oceanic Application

Lightweight and extremely durable, holding optimal aerodynamic profiles over thousands of sea miles to transfer maximum wind power into boat speed.

Profile & Aerodynamics

Computerized load modeling for perfect shape stability in all conditions.

Laminate Durability

Resistance to UV degradation, salt water corrosion, and repeated reefing.

Extreme Testing

Direct telemetry feedback sent from the Atlantic to Quantum design offices.
